Love & Reunion

Poems that hold love gently — not as conquest, but as the quiet miracle of return.

01
Love
English

Love Finds You Again

Love finds you again — not loudly, not like lightning splitting the sky, but softly… like dawn touching the edges of a sleepless room. It returns to turn your silences into something almost musical, a rhythm the heart remembers before the mind does. Not every melody is sung by birds. Some bloom quietly within — rare as the Brahma Kamal, opening only once for those who still dare to believe. Sometimes love arrives wearing another person's shadow. Sometimes it sits beside you in the strange calm of a shattered heart. And still — after distance, after storms, after all the endings we mistake for eternity — love, somehow, finds you again.

English

You're Mine

In my darkest days, I remember you. In my brightest light, I remember you. In tears of joy, I remember you. In the laughter hiding pain, I remember you. In the echo of silence, I remember you. In the noise of the world, I remember you. In the wind passing through me, I feel you. In the raindrops touching my skin, I feel you. You are mine… Not because I found love in you, but because while loving you, I found myself.

English + Hindi

Love Is a Mirror

Love is a mirror — a quiet reflection of you seen only through my eyes. An affection that waits, not in haste, but in stillness… just to adore you. We do not romance in the moments we meet, but in the spaces between — in the imagination of meeting, where every touch is felt before it exists. Love does not live in the nights we share, but in the longing for those nights to arrive. Pinjara hai yeh pyar ka — khubsurat bhi, khaufnaak bhi. Bas itna darr hai, piya… kahin iss mohabbat mein main khud se door na ho jaaun.

Category Two

Loss & Distance

The poems that sit in the silence after someone leaves — not elegies, but unfinished conversations.

05
Loss
English

Can We Be Strangers Again?

Tell me… do strangers still remain inside each other's hearts? Maybe that's what we became — tenants of old memories, paying rent by surviving inside one another. Months have passed, and yes, we still talk sometimes… but never about us. Every conversation ends with "move on," as if healing were ever that easy. You never asked whether I was okay or merely learning how to survive quietly. I thought you could read my eyes, understand the silence between my words, hear the ache I was too proud to name. But maybe pain only becomes real when it shakes through a voice. I still don't know what I am to you now — an old acquaintance, a closed chapter, a fading notification. But you… you were never temporary to me. You became like a habit the soul learns once… and the heart never truly forgets.
06
Loss
English

After You

The months went by… and yes, I grieved you. I walked away too — not because love ended, but because somewhere I had lost myself inside you. Maybe you were never mine, yet a part of me still belongs to you. I no longer fear losing you. I fear that after you, my heart may never learn to open this deeply again. I don't text every time I miss you. Only sometimes — so you remember that somewhere in this world, Aadrak still breathes your name. Dinkar ji once wrote that love weakens people, stops their growth. Maybe he was right. Because while the world moved forward, I remained standing at the doorstep of your memories. There was a time your mornings began with me, your nights dissolved into my voice. Now my words remain unopened, as though I had never existed. But love is strange. It does not disappear. Like energy, it only changes form — from touch into remembrance, from togetherness into silence. And when you once asked, "What is so special about me?" I wanted to say: Nothing. You became special because I loved you. Perhaps that is love's greatest tragedy — we pour so much meaning into someone that when they leave, we begin to feel empty ourselves.
10
Loss
English

The Last Kiss

The last kiss… still lingers like unfinished rain caught between sky and earth. It never claimed my lips, nor crowned my forehead — it arrived as a restless wind, a ghost brushing past my skin, leaving echoes instead of warmth. You ran — not like someone leaving, but like a shadow dissolving into a darker night. That kiss was not a kiss — it was a silent funeral of everything we never buried. I mistook it for a sunrise, held it like a promise of dawn — but it was the last light bleeding out of our horizon. You slipped beyond reach, like time through trembling hands… and I kept chasing reflections, in mirrors that no longer knew your face. Even if I found you again, you would stand there — familiar as a memory, but distant as a forgotten dream.

02
Code & Love
English + Hindi

First Syntax

You were my first unfinished code, the one my heart kept revisiting long after the screen went dark. My first "Hello, World" — small, uncertain, almost fragile… yet enough to rearrange the language of my existence. You were the cursor blinking at 2 AM, the silence between keystrokes, the first command my lonely soul learned by memory. Like an empty folder waiting for a lifetime of files, I saved pieces of myself inside you without even realizing it. You felt like the first page of a book I feared finishing, like a melody that lingered even after the headphones fell silent. And when you arrived, it was quiet — like rain touching sunburnt streets after a ruthless summer. Everything did not change at once. Bas zindagi ne dheere se saanslena shuru kiya. Tum mera pehla sukoon the, pehli aadat, pehla safar jahan manzil se zyada raaste khoobsurat lage.

Code & Love
English

Who Are You To Me?

Who are you to me? What if I said — you are the test case I never wish to pass, the unsolved question that keeps me awake at night. No… that feels too cold, too coded. You are the warm lemon tea I sip while the sun melts quietly into the Ganga River beside Assi Ghat. No — you are the sacred fire of Dashashwamedh Ghat, a prayer wrapped in surrender and gold. You are the tulip blooming softly inside the quiet gardens of Banaras Hindu University — not merely seen, but deeply felt. Or maybe… you are simply the part of me I could never put into words. And I — the reflection you search for in every quiet poem you read alone.

English

Beautiful Bug

What if I said — you are the test case I never wish to pass, the beautiful bug I secretly pray remains unsolved. An error I never fix, because somewhere, I have fallen in love with its existence. You are the 3 AM commit of my restless mind, the whisper hidden inside my code, not syntax — but longing. No, you are not cold logic. You are the poetry that slipped quietly between the lines of my reasoning.

09
City
English

Bengaluru — The Night

An auto drifts through sleepless roads, and Bengaluru blooms in borrowed stars, neon stitched across the night, glass towers breathing quiet light. Yet in this glittering wilderness, I do not travel alone. Somewhere between two heartbeats, I feel your hand find mine, jaan. We wander — not through streets, but through moments; through mirrored aisles and silver light, where every dress I lift becomes an excuse to search for myself in your eyes. And there, in the soft glow of your silence, I find my answer. A table waits somewhere in the night, lit not by candles, but by the warmth of us. Laughter spills like untold stories, while silence rests gently between our souls. Later, beneath a sky heavy with unsaid romance, we taste sweetness that melts too soon — ice cream on our lips, and forever on our tongues. The city rushes on, unaware. But time — time pauses for us. And in that fragile eternity, you are no longer beside me. You exist within me now, like breath, like memory, like something my soul learned before this life began. And I — no longer searching, no longer wandering, simply yours.

When Will You Come?

Yet somehow, I still find you in every cloudy sky, in every passing drizzle, in every unfinished evening. A quiet hope remains for our next meeting, for another story, another shared laugh. Ab toh baarish bhi aa gayi… Ab aap kab aaoge?

Devotion
English + Hindi

What Is Love?

What is love? It is not found only in touch, nor in endless conversations, nor in stolen glances across crowded rooms. Love is quieter than that. It is the strange peace of seeing someone smile and feeling the universe settle within you. Aaj bhi tumhare chehre ki muskurahat mere andheron ka chaand hai, ek noor jo door hokar bhi roshni deta rehta hai. Prem shayad paana nahi hota. Shayad prem — samarpan hota hai. Aur mera samarpan hi mera prem hai.
15
Devotion
English

Poetry Is Only Words

Poetry is only words… but your eyes speak louder than them all. Beneath your gaze, even the sky feels smaller. You are not merely love — you are something divine, like Krishna holding the universe within his smile. My brahmanand, my universe, my dearest — you do not need poetry. You are poetry. The echo of love in my silence, the warmth of the sun upon weary skin, the quiet darshan of an endless cosmos.

Sacred Stanzas

I will be your word in the sacred stanzas of a poem not written for gods, but for a love so profound it escapes understanding. Not worship of deity, but devotion itself — a silent offering to an unspoken bond. And somewhere within that silence, your words become my prayer, echoing softly through the corridors of longing.

Shayara Andaaz

Shayara andaaz hai, aankhon mein halki si laaj hai। Mann ek darpan sa thehra, aur us darpan mein sirf aapka hi aks hai। Khayalon mein aapka saath hai, saanson mein aapki baat hai। Aur jab aap saath hon… toh phir kya kami reh jaati hai? Hindi

Tum Lade Kyun Nahi?

Meri ek hi shikayat hai, priya — tum lade kyun nahi? Tumne kaha hamara prem dheere dheere feeka pad gaya, jaise rasam ka dhaaga apna rang chhod deta hai… Par dhaaga rang se nahi, vishwas aur prem se bandha hota hai। Prem koi rang nahi jo baarish mein beh jaaye। Prem toh woh agni hai jo bujh kar bhi raakh mein dhadakti rehti hai। Kya Radha ne apne Krishna ke virah ko haar maana tha? Kya Rukmini ne apne prem ke liye sahas nahi bheja tha? Kya Meera Bai ne mahal chhodkar bhi apne prem ko nahi paaya tha? Kya Parvati ki tapasya adhuri reh gayi thi? Kya Sita ne vanvaas mein apne Rama ka haath chhod diya tha? Toh phir tum kyun thak gaye? Kyun ruk gaye? Kyun chhod gaye? Yeh koi kavita nahi। Yeh main hoon — jo maine tumhe samarpit kar diya tha, jaise diya apni lau ko samarpit hota hai। Agar prem sach tha… toh ek baar aur lad lete। Duniya se nahi, mere liye hi sahi। Par tum… lade kyun nahi?
Hindi Ghazal

Tujhe Maine Maanga Hai

Tujhe maine har dua, har ibaadat mein maanga hai, Teri yaadon ko rooh mein chupke se basa rakha hai। Shayad tere liye main sirf ek kahaani tha, Par maine tujhe apni zindagi ka fasana bana rakha hai। Woh din aaj bhi dhadkan tez kar deta hai, Jab teri aankhon ne mujhe khamoshi se chhoo liya tha। Tab se azaad hokar bhi main azaad na reh saka, Teri mohabbat ka pinjara hi mera jahaan ban gaya। Tere liye hoga main ek pagal aashiq, Par meri deewangi mein sirf sachchai hai। Bas rab se itni si dua hai — tujhe har khushi, har kamyabi naseeb ho। Aur agar meri mohabbat sach hai… toh meri manzil ka raasta phir tere hi paas aaye।

Traveling Back Through the Lanes of Memory

Traveling back through the lanes of memory, I walk the streets where your laughter still echoes. The protagonist was never the sunrise — it was the quiet dusk where your shadow met mine, and time forgot to move. In the stillness of my soul you linger — a gentle ghost in every thought. Let us close this chapter where it began… with a dream we built together, and a morning that woke without you.

Quiet Modern Longing

Maybe it is the inertia of this moment, slowing the velocity of two hearts once certain of each other. Tera ek message… bas ek, aur is chehre par phir se woh roshni aa jaati hai। Please reply, jaan — before your adrak turns cold in waiting.

Literary & Intimate

The World For You, My Kayanat

Tu Meera ban, meri raah dekhna, Main Krishna ban, sabka priya sahi — par prem mein meri ek hi Ganga hai। Jo har jagah behti hai, par jiska poojan sirf Kashi aur Haridwar mein hota hai। Tu mera woh kinara ban, jise paakar main Dashashwamedh Ghat sa pavitra ho jaaun। Jahan tera sparsh mil jaaye, wahan har teerth chhota lage, aur mann mein shaanti ka sangam utar aaye। Kyunki kabhi-kabhi anth hi hota hai ek naye geet ka mukhda, ek nayi kahani ka pehla panna। Bas yunhi sang rehna mera — chahe baadal ho ya dhoop gehri। Aur tu bhi ek kavita ban ja, jise har baar padho… toh nayi lage।

Between Silences

These poems were written in the language of longing — in English, Hindi, and Urdu — across the streets of Varanasi, Bengaluru, and the unnamed cities of the heart. They are not finished. They are not meant to be.
